# Four-edge residuals in L-meta-17625

With exactly two dirty ANDs, no centerless top and exactly two exceptional ORs, a four-edge residual in a least top-form binary AND/OR circuit for a bipartite, four-cycle-free graph must be a star. All four edges share a vertex. Existence of such a circuit remains open. [Common-center proof](src/RemainingGeometry.lean) and [matching bound](src/DeskMatching.lean).
